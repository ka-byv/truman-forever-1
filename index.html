Truman walked out.
Would you have done the same?

By Kalita · September 10, 2026

For thirty years, Truman Burbank lived what appeared to be an ordinary life. He had a home, a job, a wife, friends and a peaceful community.

There was just one problem: none of it was real.

Truman eventually discovered that his entire life had been turned into a television show without his knowledge or consent.

Was Truman really free?

His decision to leave Seahaven therefore becomes much more than simply walking through a door. It represents his decision to choose an uncertain reality over a comfortable illusion.

But what would you have done?
